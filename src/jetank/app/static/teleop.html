Drag the pad, or use W A S D / arrow keys. Space or STOP halts the robot. The robot also stops by itself if this page closes, is hidden, or loses the connection, and when the video freezes while it is moving. Gripper: O opens, C closes, R releases (goes limp). Closing the page or losing the connection releases it. Arm: hold F/G, H/J, K/L or U/N (camera) to move a joint; it stops when the key is let go and keeps holding its pose. Teach: pose the arm and gripper around a toy, fill in the labels and press Take pose; the robot saves the measured joints, the gripper and the camera image. Keys do nothing while you type in a field. Scenes: training images for the toy detector. Press Search pose (the arm moves to the taught search pose), fill in room and light, then Take scene (P) or Start auto, which saves one image every few seconds while you drive, until you stop it or its time limit is reached. Untick "animals in view" for scenes without toy animals (other toys, socks, cables are welcome there). Auto keeps the labels it was started with. Sleep (Z) stops the tracks, moves the arm home and lets arm and gripper go limp; the robot also does this by itself after a while without arm or gripper commands. Moving the arm or gripper wakes it up.
